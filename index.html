What is CLPS?

Commercial Lunar Payload Services is a NASA program that buys rides to the Moon from private companies. The companies build and fly the landers, and NASA's science and technology instruments ride along. It helps prepare for future crewed missions.

How to read this

The Sun and Earth are always close to the horizon near the poles. Power needs sunlight, and direct radio contact needs Earth above the horizon. Telemetry covers the next 30 days from today.
Estimates use simplified orbit formulas and a flat horizon. Real terrain and mission data will change the results.
