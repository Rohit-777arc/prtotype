🌙 Lunar South Pole Sun & Earth Planner

Compare candidate landing sites and dates: Sun/Earth elevation vs. the horizon, power and direct-to-Earth windows. PROTOTYPE.

Sun Earth Both visible

Method: low-precision lunar libration and subsolar-point formulas (accurate to roughly a few tenths of a degree), so treat results as indicative. At the poles the Sun stays within about 1.5° of the horizon, so small errors matter. The horizon mask is a single flat angle you set; real terrain needs LOLA/DEM horizon data from the official challenge datasets. Site coordinates are approximate. Validate against NASA SPICE or JPL Horizons before relying on any number.
